Refund & Dispute Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

FileLens sells downloadable desktop software and software licenses.

14-day refund requests

If you are dissatisfied with FileLens Pro or the software does not work as reasonably expected on a supported Windows system, contact us within 14 calendar days of purchase at support@advoryn.com with the purchase email and a short description of the issue.

We will review eligible requests promptly and may ask for basic diagnostic information needed to understand a technical problem. We do not require you to send the contents of private log or CSV files.

Payment-provider support

For purchases processed through Stripe Managed Payments, customers may also request transaction support or a refund through the support options provided in their payment receipt or Link account. Stripe may process refunds in accordance with its Managed Payments policies and applicable law.

Exceptions

We may decline a request where there is evidence of fraud, abuse, chargeback misuse, or a request outside the stated period, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

Disputes

Please contact us first so we can attempt to resolve product or licensing issues. This policy does not limit rights available under applicable consumer-protection law.
