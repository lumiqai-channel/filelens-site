FileLens Support

Support for FileLens Free and FileLens Pro

Email: support@advoryn.com

License activation

After purchasing FileLens Pro, the license key is sent to the email address used at checkout. In FileLens, open Help → License, paste the key, and choose Activate Pro.

A FileLens Pro license supports one purchaser on up to three active devices. If you reach the device limit, deactivate FileLens Pro on a device you no longer use before activating another one.

Payment or license email issues

If payment succeeded but you did not receive a license email, check Spam/Junk first, then email support with the purchase email address. Do not send payment-card numbers or other sensitive financial information.

Refunds

See the Refund & Dispute Policy. A full refund revokes the associated FileLens Pro license.

Privacy and diagnostics

Files opened in FileLens are processed locally. If support requests diagnostics, use the application's local diagnostics feature and review the information before sharing it.

Compatibility

FileLens 1.0 is designed for Windows 10/11 x64. Performance with very large files depends on storage speed, free disk space, available memory, and the operation being performed.
